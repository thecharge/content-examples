# Adaptive liquid board delivery record

Objective: extend the supplied adaptive liquid network in a separate CPU folder. Preserve the original reference and its contextual learning. Deliver code, saved models, comparable evidence and detailed documents on main. No GitHub Actions.

Implemented: named inputs; original contextual feedback alongside structural trials; learned expert action values and bounded reinforcement replay; input grouping; peer and encoding-neuron splits; full liquid child boards; exact weight sharing with independent context state; objective retirement; growable and prunable delta heads; feedback guards; configurable limits; function-preserving growth; static-shape fitting, warmup and early stopping; versioned snapshots; event journals and point-in-time recovery; scratch training, fine-tuning and model inspection.

Verified locally: 36 adaptive behavioral tests passed in 44.469 seconds; all 20 unchanged reference tests passed in 0.209 seconds. All three final house receipts passed source, archive, checkpoint, artifact, score, gate, chronology and restored-topology checks. Controlled child-growth, neuron-split and retirement receipts passed independent source, timing, score and gate checks.

Measured results: the new learner reproduces H5 balanced accuracy after day 14 of 82.61%, 87.50% and 89.86%, matching the original contextual learner. H6 outcomes remain mixed. The house streams retained one weight-learning candidate and no structural changes. Controlled streams separately establish actual structural adaptation; they do not establish additional occupancy gains. RESULTS.md gives every range and comparator, rolling curves and retained actions.

Documentation: README.md provides the entry point and requirement map. ARCHITECTURE.md gives Mermaid diagrams, equations and component responsibilities. CONFIGURATION.md defines every new operating setting. OPERATIONS.md explains inspection, reproduction, training, snapshots and recovery. reporting/generate.py rebuilds the report from verified predictions and fingerprints its artifacts.

The original verified-liquid-board implementation, archives and unrelated repositories were preserved. The saved runtime models retain pending labels and unfinished trials explicitly. There is no claim of monotonic accuracy or a measured benefit from larger topology on these homes.
